From the Editor — one opinion, weekly

The measuring cups are the franchise. At 33.7% margin and a 98% hold on the Buy Box, the Set Pro would bear a fifty-cent price rise without flinching — worth roughly $207 a week. That is the cheapest money available to this business.

The paracord, by contrast, is demanding attention it has not earned: a lost Buy Box, a leaking margin, and a shelf running down. Deal with the hijacker first; the other two problems are only worth solving if the listing is yours again.

The category is growing 14% a year. The plan is not complicated: protect the cups, decide on the cord, and keep sixty days of stock between you and bad luck.
